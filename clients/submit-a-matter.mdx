To send a matter, sign in, choose a service and describe your situation. It is free. The firm sends a quote only if it accepts your matter.

## Before you start

- An email address you can open.
- Any files that explain your situation, such as a contract or a letter.
- A browser that allows pop-ups from Orbid. **New matter** opens in a new tab.

When you start, a bar at the top shows five steps: Choose service, Describe needs, Review details, Verify identity and View progress.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the link from your firm. Enter your email and then the code Orbid sends you. Google, Microsoft and WeChat sign-in say "Coming soon", so use email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select New matter">
    On your home page, select **New matter**. Orbid opens "Start a matter" in a new tab. Keep working in that tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose service">
    Search for the closest service, such as "set up a company" or "NDA". If nothing fits, choose "Something else" and describe it in a few words.
  </Step>
  {/* screenshot: intake-select-service | Matter step "Select a service" with the search box filled with "NDA", one result selected and the "What this usually involves" panel open; demo client "Demo GmbH" | role=client | url=/orbid/intake/new */}
  <Step title="Describe needs">
    Orbid asks guided questions one at a time, such as "Question 3 of 8". It is not a free chat, so you only answer what you see. The questions may cover the country or region you come from and go to, and whether you are a company, an individual or an agent acting for someone else. Add your files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review details">
    Read the summary Orbid writes and fix anything that is wrong.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify identity">
    Upload your identity documents. See [Upload identity documents](/clients/identity-documents). Then select **Submit and lock**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View progress">
    After you submit, this step shows where your matter stands. See [Track your matter](/clients/track-your-matter).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The guide next to each service is only a rough idea. It is not legal advice. The lawyer decides.
</Note>

## Common questions

**Does it cost anything to submit?** No. You pay later, after the firm accepts and sends a [quote](/clients/quotes-and-payment).

**Can I change my answers after I submit?** Not while the lawyer is reviewing. If the firm declines, you can [edit and resubmit](/clients/resubmit-a-declined-matter).

**Can I stop and come back?** Yes. Unfinished matters appear under "You have unfinished matters". Select **Continue**.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Could not open a new tab. Please allow pop-ups for this site and retry.” | Allow pop-ups for this site in your browser settings, then select **New matter** again. |
| “Nothing matches” | Choose “Something else” and describe your matter briefly. |
| “Please read before uploading” | Read the notice. If you agree, select **Confirm and upload**. If not, ask your firm. Nothing is sent until you confirm. |
| Your invitation link does not work | Ask your firm to send you a new link. |
| “Could not save. Please try again.” | Try again in a moment. |
