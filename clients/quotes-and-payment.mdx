After the firm accepts your matter, it sends a fixed price. Check it and select **Confirm & pay**. The lawyer starts work once you have paid.

## Before you start

- Wait until your matter shows a quote. You see "Your lawyer set a fixed price. Confirm and pay to start the work."
- If your company limits who can pay, make sure your account is allowed to. Otherwise ask your company administrator. An approver or finance member can pay.

## What should I check?

A quote card shows the details.

| Item | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed price | The total you will pay |
| Currency | EUR, USD or CNY |
| Valid until | The last day. The card also says how many days are left, such as “5 days left”. Usually 14 days; the firm can set 1 to 60. |
| Version | A newer quote replaces an older one. |

If the date has passed, the card says "Expired". See "If something goes wrong" below.

## What do the status labels mean?

Your matter shows one of these labels.

| Label | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Not started | You have not sent the matter yet. |
| Awaiting review | Submitted. We are arranging a lawyer to review it. |
| Accepted | The firm accepted your matter. A quote follows. |
| Declined | The firm declined. You can edit and resubmit. |
| Quoted | Your lawyer set a fixed price. Confirm and pay to start the work. |
| Paid | Paid. Your lawyer has started work. |
| Returned for changes | The firm sent your matter back. Make the changes and send it again. |
| Cancelled | The matter was cancelled. Nothing more happens. |

## How do I pay?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the matter">
    Select the matter that has a quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the quote">
    Make sure it is the latest one and has not run out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Select **Confirm & pay** and follow the steps for your payment method.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The price is fixed. You cannot negotiate or decline it in Orbid. To change the terms, contact the firm. It can send a new quote.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “This quote expired. Your lawyer will send a new one.” | Wait for the new quote, or ask the firm. |
| “The quote was just updated” | Read the new quote, then try again. |
| “Online payment is not available yet” | Contact the firm to arrange payment. |
| Your payment failed | Check your quote and payment status, then try again. If you are unsure, ask the firm first so you do not pay twice. |
| You paid but nothing changed | Do not pay again. Contact the firm. |
| “Your role does not allow this” | Ask your company administrator to pay or to change your role. |
