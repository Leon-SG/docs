Your lawyer must confirm who you are before starting work. You upload identity documents in the Verify step, as PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC files up to 25 MB each.

## Before you start

- Your identity documents, or your company's, ready on your device.
- A few minutes. You also sign a short declaration.

## What can I upload?

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| File types | PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC |
| Size of one file | 25 MB |
| Files for one item | 10 |
| Total for one matter | 300 MB |

Empty files and password-protected PDFs are not accepted. Renaming a file does not change its type.

## How do I upload them?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Verify step">
    Orbid shows a checklist. Every item marked **Required** needs at least one file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a file for each item">
    Select **Upload file** next to an item. To swap a file, select **Remove** and upload another.
  </Step>
  {/* screenshot: intake-verify-upload | Verify step with a checklist of three required items; the first shows an uploaded file "passport.pdf", the others show "Upload file"; demo client "Demo GmbH" | role=client | url=/orbid/intake/new */}
  <Step title="Sign">
    Read the consent text and type the name of the person who signs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Select **Submit and lock**. If a required item has no file, Orbid says which one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why does the firm need this?

The lawyer must know who the client is before taking on the work. Some matters need a full check, which can include the people who own the company. Orbid shows which check applies, and the lawyer decides what is needed.

## What am I agreeing to?

You confirm the documents are genuine. You also agree that the firm may handle your personal data. This can mean sending it to Germany under China's data rules (PIPL), or handling it under the GDPR. If you do not agree, talk to your firm before you upload.

## What happens after I upload?

A lawyer looks at each item by hand and marks it as passed. The firm cannot send you a quote until every required item has passed. If the lawyer needs more, you see a request in your workspace. See [Track your matter](/clients/track-your-matter).

<Info>
Orbid's AI may point out a problem, such as an expired or unclear document. A lawyer always makes the final decision.
</Info>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “File too large (max 25 MB each)” | Make the file smaller or upload fewer pages per file. |
| “This matter's files exceed 300 MB in total” | Remove files you do not need. |
| “Unsupported type” | Save the file as PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC. |
| “The PDF is encrypted” | Remove the password and upload it again. |
| “This document seems to have expired” | Upload a valid document. |
| “The file is hard to read” | Take a new photo or scan. |
| “Some required items have no file yet” | Add a file to every item marked **Required**. |
