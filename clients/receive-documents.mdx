You see your documents after the lawyer approves and delivers them. Open the matter, download the files and confirm that you received them.

## Before you start

- Your matter must show a delivery. Until then, you see only your own uploaded files.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the matter">
    Select the matter that shows "Delivery to confirm".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your files">
    A delivery can hold several files. Select **Download document** for each one.
  </Step>
  {/* screenshot: client-delivery-files | Matter with a delivery of two files, each with "Download document", and the receipt box below it; demo client "Demo GmbH" | role=client | url=/orbid/client */}
  <Step title="Confirm receipt">
    Type your name, tick "I have received and accept this deliverable" and select **Confirm receipt**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your invoice">
    After you confirm, select **Download invoice PDF**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What about translations?

For some deliveries, you also get a translated copy next to the formal document. Ask your lawyer which version applies to you.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot see your files yet | The lawyer has not delivered yet. Wait for the delivery. |
| “This document is temporarily unavailable” | Contact your lawyer. |
| “Unable to preview this file” | Download the file instead. |
| “Receipt could not be recorded” | Try again. If it still fails, contact your lawyer. |
| Your invoice is missing | Confirm receipt first. The invoice comes after. |
