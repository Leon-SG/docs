Your workspace shows each matter, its progress, your messages and anything the firm needs from you. Open a matter to see your next step.

## How do I read the progress bar?

| Stage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Intake | You are still describing the matter. |
| KYC | You are uploading identity documents. |
| Lawyer review | The lawyer is reading your matter. |
| Quote | The firm accepted and sends or has sent a price. |
| Payment | A quote is waiting for you to confirm and pay. |
| In progress | You paid and the lawyer is working. |
| Delivered | Your documents are ready. |

Your home page sorts matters into three groups: **Needs you**, **In progress** and **Delivered**. Matters that need you appear first.

Each matter also carries a small chip that says what is happening now.

| Chip | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Describing | You are still writing your answers. |
| Scope to confirm | Check and confirm what the work covers. |
| Waiting for you | A step is waiting for you. |
| Waiting for a lawyer | The firm is arranging a lawyer. Nothing is needed from you. |
| Lawyer review | The lawyer is reading your matter. |
| Delivery to confirm | Your documents have arrived. Confirm that you received them. |
| Delivered | The matter is finished. |

## How do I write to the firm?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the matter">
    Select the matter in your list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets your message">
    Under "To", pick XiaoOu (Orbid's AI assistant) or your lawyer. Sending a message does not mean the firm has accepted your matter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send">
    Type your message and send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Orbid's AI is an aid. Ask your lawyer if you are unsure about anything.
</Note>

## What if the firm asks for more documents?

The lawyer can ask you to upload some identity documents again. Those items go back to "to upload". Upload new files for them. Your old files stay on record but do not count as passed.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot see a matter | Sign in with the account of the company that owns the matter. Your company may limit a matter to certain people. Ask your administrator. |
| A matter says “Waiting for a lawyer” | Wait. The firm is arranging a lawyer. Nothing is needed from you. |
| The matter was declined | See [Resubmit a declined matter](/clients/resubmit-a-declined-matter). |
