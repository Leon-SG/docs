If the firm declines your matter, you can fix it and send it again. You can do this up to two times.

## Before you start

- Read the reason the lawyer gave. It tells you what to fix.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the declined matter">
    Find the reason shown under "Reason".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Edit and resubmit">
    This sends the matter back to you so you can change it.
  </Step>
  {/* screenshot: client-declined-edit | Declined matter showing the lawyer's reason and the "Edit and resubmit" button; demo client "Demo GmbH" | role=client | url=/orbid/client */}
  <Step title="Fix your matter">
    Correct your answers and add any missing files. Every required identity item needs a file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it again">
    Type your name to sign and select **Resubmit to lawyer**. The lawyer then reviews it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How many times can I resubmit?

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Resubmissions for one matter | 2 |

After the second time, you see "You have used both resubmits". Contact the lawyer for help.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Edit and resubmit** button | You may have used both resubmits. Contact your firm. |
| “Some required KYC items have no file” | Add files to the items marked **Required**, then resubmit. |
