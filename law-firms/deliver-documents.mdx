To deliver, check your files, select **Deliver** and confirm in the **Confirm delivery** window. The client sees your files only after you do.

## Before you start

- The files are finished and you have read them.
- You know which version the client should get (see the table below).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check every file">
    Open each file, including reports, and read it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to handle comments">
    Comments are handled in the export menu. Choose **Keep comments (n)** or **Remove all comments**, where n is the number of comments. Then choose which version to export. See the table below.
  </Step>
  {/* screenshot: counsel-delivery-comments | Export menu with "Keep comments (4)" and "Remove all comments" and the two versions, Lawyer version and Client version; demo matter with 4 comments | role=lawyer | url=/orbid/counsel */}
  <Step title="Select Deliver">
    Select **Deliver**. The **Confirm delivery** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Enter **Your name** and tick "I am authorised to release this draft to the client". Then select **Deliver**, or **Cancel** to go back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Export choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Keep comments (n) | The comments stay in the file. |
| Remove all comments | The file has no comments. |
| Lawyer version (internal + both) | Keeps internal notes and notes meant for both you and the client. |
| Client version (client + both) | Keeps only notes meant for the client and notes meant for both. |

<Warning>
If any high-risk findings are still unresolved, a warning tells you how many. You can go back to **Review** or continue. Read them first.
</Warning>

<Warning>
The lawyer version includes internal notes. Do not send it to the client.
</Warning>

## What does the client see?

The client sees the files in their workspace and confirms receipt. The matter then shows "Delivery to confirm" until they do.

<Warning>
Orbid's AI is an aid. You must review and approve everything you deliver.
</Warning>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A warning about unresolved high-risk findings | Go back to **Review** and deal with them, or continue if you have checked them. |
| You cannot confirm | Check that you entered **Your name** and ticked the authorisation box. |
| “Action failed” | Try again in a moment. |
