After you accept a matter, write a quote and select **Send quote**. The client sees it only after you send it. A saved draft stays private.

## Before you start

- Accept the matter first.
- To send the quote, every required identity item must have passed. You can write and save a draft before that.

## What can I set?

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Valid days | 14 by default. Choose 1 to 60. |
| Currency | EUR, USD or CNY |
| Tax treatment | Standard VAT, reverse charge or exempt |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the work and the price">
    Open the accepted matter. Add each item with a quantity and a net unit price. Pick the currency and tax treatment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note and valid days">
    The note is shown to the client. Choose how many days the quote stays valid.
  </Step>
  {/* screenshot: counsel-quote-editor | Quote editor with two line items, currency EUR, 14 valid days, total shown and the "Save draft" and "Send quote" buttons; demo client "Demo GmbH" | role=lawyer | url=/orbid/counsel/engagements */}
  <Step title="Save a draft if you need to">
    Select **Save draft**. The client cannot see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Select **Send quote** and confirm. The client can now pay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Can I change a quote I sent?

No. You cannot take back a sent quote. Send a new one instead. The new quote replaces the old one. Clients cannot decline a fixed price. They contact you if they want changes.

## When does the work start?

After the client pays. Then the matter workbench is open.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Accept the matter before sending a quote” | Accept the matter first. |
| “Reverse charge needs the client's VAT ID” | Enter the client's VAT ID, or choose another tax treatment. |
| You cannot send the quote | Check that every required identity item has passed. |
| The matter is paid but you do not see it | Reload the page. If it is still missing, contact support. |
