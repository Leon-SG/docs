The matter workbench holds the chat, facts, files and documents for one paid matter. You open it after the client pays.

## Before you start

- The client has paid the quote. Until then, the matter stays in Engagement review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the matter">
    In Engagement review, select a paid matter and then **Open matter workbench**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the facts, files and messages">
    Read them before you ask the AI to analyze or draft anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a document">
    Check which file is open. It may be the original, your edited version or a draft the AI wrote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and decide">
    Keep the changes you agree with. Then go on to [deliver the documents](/law-firms/deliver-documents).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What is in each tab?

The workbench has these tabs. **KYC** appears only when the matter needs identity checks, and **Asks** only while you have open requests to the client.

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Files | See the client's files and open a document. |
| Chat | Read and write messages with the client. |
| Agent | Ask Orbid's AI to analyze or draft. |
| Review | Read the findings and decide which to keep. |
| KYC | Check the client's identity documents. |
| Facts | Read the facts collected about the matter. |
| Asks | Follow the documents you asked the client for. |
| Deliveries | Deliver finished files to the client. |

## What is "your edited version"?

The original is the file the client gave you. Your edited version is your own copy, where you make changes. A draft written by the AI is a third thing. Check which one you are editing before you save or deliver.

## How do I ask for more identity documents?

Ask the client to upload the items you name. Those items go back to "to upload". Earlier files stay on record. If you change your mind, check the items' status again, because they do not go back automatically.

<Warning>
Orbid's AI is an aid. Check its findings, drafts and sources yourself. You are responsible for what you deliver.
</Warning>

Use **Request documents** in Engagement review.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A file will not load or preview | Download the original and try again later. |
| A draft did not finish | Check your edited version is saved, then try again. |
| A source is missing or wrong | Fix it before you deliver. |
