## Your Role in Orbid AI

Medical Affairs teams—including Medical Affairs Leads, MSLs, and Scientific Engagement Managers—use Orbid AI to manage KOL relationships, execute scientific events, and monitor regulatory intelligence.

<Info>
This guide maps your daily workflows to Orbid AI features. For detailed instructions, follow the links to specific guides.
</Info>

## Your Key Workflows

### 1. KOL Relationship Management

Build and maintain relationships with Key Opinion Leaders.

- **Add a new KOL to your database**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **KOL** → **New KOL**
  - Guide: [Create a KOL](/guides/kol/create-kol)
- **Import KOLs from spreadsheet**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **KOL** → **Import CSV**
  - Guide: [Import KOLs](/guides/kol/import-kols-csv)
- **View KOL profile and history**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **KOL** → Select a profile
  - Guide: [KOL Profile & Insights](/guides/kol/kol-profile-and-insights)
- **Log an interaction (call, meeting, email)**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **KOL** → Profile → **Quick Actions**
  - Guide: [KOL Management](/guides/kol)
- **Tier and classify KOLs**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **KOL** → Profile → Edit **Tier** field
  - Guide: [KOL Tiering Methodology](/best-practices/kol-tiering-methodology)
- **Get AI recommendations for a KOL**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **KOL** → Profile → **AI Insights** tab
  - Guide: [KOL Profile & Insights](/guides/kol/kol-profile-and-insights)

**Quick Actions for logging interactions:**
- **Made Contact**: Phone calls or direct contact
- **Sent Email**: Email communications
- **Meeting**: Scheduled or completed meetings
- **Follow-up**: Mark for future action

### 2. Scientific Event Execution

Plan and run advisory boards, symposiums, and investigator meetings.

- **Create a new event**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Events** → **New Event**
  - Guide: [Events Dashboard](/guides/events)
- **Add KOLs as attendees**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Events** → Event → **Attendees** tab
  - Guide: [Event Details](/guides/events/event-details)
- **Send invitations and track RSVPs**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Events** → Event → **Invitations** tab
  - Guide: [Event Details](/guides/events/event-details)
- **Build the event agenda**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Events** → Event → **Agenda** tab
  - Guide: [Event Details](/guides/events/event-details)
- **Generate materials (agenda PDF, attendee list)**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Events** → Event → **Materials** tab
  - Guide: [Event Details](/guides/events/event-details)
- **Set up automated reminders**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Events** → Event → **Automation** tab
  - Guide: [Event Details](/guides/events/event-details)
- **Send post-event thank you**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Events** → Event → **Automation** tab
  - Guide: [Event Details](/guides/events/event-details)

**Event types you'll commonly use:**
- Advisory Board
- Symposium
- Investigator Meeting
- Training / Workshop
- Webinar

### 3. Intelligence & Research

Monitor regulatory changes and generate research reports.

- **Monitor regulatory signals**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Signals** → Dashboard
  - Guide: [Signals Overview](/guides/signals)
- **Filter signals by authority (FDA, EMA, etc.)**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Signals** → Filters
  - Guide: [Signal Details](/guides/signals/signal-details)
- **Assess product impact of a signal**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Signals** → Signal detail → Product Relevance
  - Guide: [Signal Details](/guides/signals/signal-details)
- **Generate a regulatory intelligence report**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Investigations** → **New Investigation**
  - Guide: [Create Investigation](/guides/investigations/create-investigation)
- **Create market or competitive analysis**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Investigations** → Choose template
  - Guide: [Create Investigation](/guides/investigations/create-investigation)

**Signal sources relevant to Medical Affairs:**
- FDA (US regulatory updates)
- EMA (EU regulatory updates)
- NMPA (China regulatory updates)
- Clinical trial registries
- Scientific publications

### 4. AI-Assisted Workflows

Use Orbid AI AI to accelerate common tasks.

- **Discover KOLs by specialty**
  - How to Access: `/discover [count] [specialty] KOLs`
  - What It Does: Find new KOLs matching criteria
- **Draft event invitations**
  - How to Access: `/event remind [event name]`
  - What It Does: Generate reminder emails
- **Send post-event follow-up**
  - How to Access: `/event thankyou [event name]`
  - What It Does: Generate thank you emails
- **Product outreach to KOLs**
  - How to Access: `/outreach product [product] to [specialty] KOLs`
  - What It Does: Draft product introduction

Access these via the **AI** module or type `/` in the command bar.

## Quick Start Checklist

Get set up in Orbid AI as a Medical Affairs user:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import your KOL database">
    Go to **KOL** → **Import CSV** and upload your existing KOL spreadsheet. [Guide](/guides/kol/import-kols-csv)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tier your top KOLs">
    Open each KOL profile and set the **Tier** (1, 2, or 3) and **Engagement Level**. [Framework](/best-practices/kol-tiering-methodology)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first event">
    Go to **Events** → **New Event** and set up an upcoming advisory board or symposium. [Guide](/guides/events)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up signal monitoring">
    Go to **Signals** and review the dashboard. Filter by FDA/EMA to see regulatory updates. [Guide](/guides/signals)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your products">
    Go to **Products** → **New Product** to add your device portfolio for signal correlation. [Guide](/guides/products)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Metrics You'll Track

- **KOL engagement level distribution**
  - Where to Find: **KOL** list filters
  - Why It Matters: Balance your portfolio across engagement levels
- **Event RSVP rates**
  - Where to Find: **Events** → Event dashboard
  - Why It Matters: Measure invitation effectiveness
- **Invitation funnel (sent → opened → responded)**
  - Where to Find: **Events** → Event → Invitations
  - Why It Matters: Optimize outreach timing
- **Signal impact on products**
  - Where to Find: **Signals** → Product filter
  - Why It Matters: Prioritize regulatory response
- **Investigation output**
  - Where to Find: **Investigations** list
  - Why It Matters: Track research productivity
