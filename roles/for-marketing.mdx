## Your Role in Orbid AI

Marketing and Business Development teams use Orbid AI to run multi-channel campaigns, create AI-assisted content, track market trends, and identify business opportunities.

<Info>
This guide maps your daily workflows to Orbid AI features. For detailed instructions, follow the links to specific guides.
</Info>

## Your Key Workflows

### 1. Campaign Management

Create and execute email campaigns to engage KOLs and customers.

- **Create a new campaign**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Campaigns** → **New Campaign**
  - Guide: [Campaigns Overview](/guides/campaign)
- **Choose campaign type**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Campaign → Select type (Product Update, Newsletter, etc.)
  - Guide: [Campaigns Overview](/guides/campaign)
- **Build your audience**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Campaign → **Audience** tab
  - Guide: [Campaigns Overview](/guides/campaign)
- **Select email template**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Campaign → **Template** selection
  - Guide: [Email Templates](/guides/campaign/email-templates)
- **Personalize with variables**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Campaign → Edit email content
  - Guide: [Personalization Variables](/guides/campaign/personalization-variables)
- **Preview and test**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Campaign → **Preview**
  - Guide: [Preview & Test Emails](/guides/campaign/preview-and-testing)
- **Schedule or send**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Campaign → **Schedule** settings
  - Guide: [Campaigns Overview](/guides/campaign)
- **Track performance**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Campaign → **Analytics**
  - Guide: [Campaigns Overview](/guides/campaign)

**Campaign types:**
- **Product Update**: Announce new features or improvements
- **Event Invitation**: Invite audience to events
- **New Product Launch**: Major product announcements
- **Newsletter**: Regular updates and content digest
- **Custom**: Flexible format for any purpose

**Email tracking metrics:**
- Delivery rate
- Open rate
- Click rate
- Unsubscribe rate
- Bounce rate

Generate AI-assisted marketing content.

- **Create a new article**

- **Generate with AI assistance**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Article → AI content generation
  - Guide: [Create Article](/guides/press/create-article)
- **Refine and improve content**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Article → **Improve** tools
  - Guide: [Improve Article](/guides/press/improve-article)
- **Add SEO keywords**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Article → Keyword settings
  - Guide: [Improve Article](/guides/press/improve-article)
- **Publish or export**
  - Where in Orbid AI: Article → Publish/Download
  - Guide: [Press Overview](/guides/press)

**Content types you can create:**
- Blog articles
- Press releases
- Product announcements
- Thought leadership pieces
- Case studies

### 4. KOL Engagement for Marketing

Coordinate with Medical Affairs on KOL outreach.

- **View KOL database**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **KOL** → Dashboard
  - Guide: [KOL Management](/guides/kol)
- **Filter KOLs for campaigns**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **KOL** → Filters (tier, specialty)
  - Guide: [KOL Management](/guides/kol)
- **Add KOLs to campaign audience**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **Campaigns** → Audience → Add KOLs
  - Guide: [Campaigns Overview](/guides/campaign)
- **View KOL engagement history**
  - Where in Orbid AI: **KOL** → Profile → Activity
  - Guide: [KOL Profile](/guides/kol/kol-profile-and-insights)

<Warning>
Coordinate with Medical Affairs before reaching out to Tier 1 KOLs to ensure messaging alignment.
</Warning>

### 5. AI-Assisted Workflows

Use Orbid AI AI to accelerate marketing tasks.

- **Create newsletter campaign**
  - How to Access: `/campaign newsletter to [audience]`
  - What It Does: Draft newsletter content
- **Launch product campaign**
  - How to Access: `/campaign launch [product]`
  - What It Does: Generate product launch sequence
- **Cold outreach to KOLs**
  - How to Access: `/outreach cold to [specialty] KOLs`
  - What It Does: Draft introduction emails
- **Follow-up with contacts**
  - How to Access: `/outreach followup`
  - What It Does: Generate follow-up sequences

Access these via the **AI** module or type `/` in the command bar.

## Quick Start Checklist

Get set up in Orbid AI as a Marketing user:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your first campaign">
    Go to **Campaigns** → **New Campaign** and select a campaign type. [Guide](/guides/campaign)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your audience">
    Add KOLs from the database or import external contacts to your campaign audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up email templates">
    Explore available templates and customize for your brand. [Guide](/guides/campaign/email-templates)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your products">
    Go to **Products** → **New Product** to add your product portfolio for campaign association. [Guide](/guides/products)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore market trends">
    Go to **Trends** to see funding and procurement activity in your target markets. [Guide](/guides/trends)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Campaign Planning Calendar

Orbid AI helps you plan campaigns around events and product milestones:

- **4 weeks before event**
  - Campaign Type: Event Save the Date
  - Audience: Target attendees
- **2 weeks before event**
  - Campaign Type: Event Invitation
  - Audience: Target attendees
- **1 week before event**
  - Campaign Type: Event Reminder
  - Audience: Registered attendees
- **Post-event**
  - Campaign Type: Thank You + Content
  - Audience: Attendees
- **Product launch**
  - Campaign Type: Launch Announcement
  - Audience: Full list
- **Monthly**
  - Campaign Type: Newsletter
  - Audience: Subscribers
