# What is Orbid not?

Orbid is **not** a generic LLM chat wrapper, **not** a guarantee of bid award, and **not** a replacement for regulatory counsel or authorized signatories. It is a native **bid-operations agent** for medical-device tenders—scan, match, fill, and deliver assistance with **human ownership** of the submission.

## Clear limits

- It does **not** guarantee a win. Award decisions stay with the buyer.  
- It does **not** replace legal or regulatory counsel. Registration and liability stay with your experts.  
- It does **not** fully unsupervised submit. Residual and commercial risk stay with you.  
- This help center is **not** the application source code.

### What it is

A workflow agent that makes medical bid ops faster and more reviewable. See [What is Orbid AI](/guides/what-is-orbid).
