# What is a compliance matrix?

A **compliance matrix** is a structured table that maps tender requirements to how your product responds—typically meet, partial, or gap—with notes or evidence. Orbid AI can **draft** a matrix after match; humans own status labels, deviation language, and whether the pack is submission-ready.

## Details

- **Meet** — Spec truly covers requirement
- **Partial** — Documented deviation / alternate
- **Gap** — Strategy: no-bid, change product, or letter
