# Does Orbid AI submit bids?

**No.** Orbid AI helps scan tenders, match products, fill forms, and draft deliverables. It does **not** submit packages to the buyer portal, award contracts, set final prices, or replace legal/regulatory sign-off. Humans own go/no-go, commercial terms, and submission.

## What Orbid can do

- Structure requirements and gaps  
- Draft filled Excel and matrices  
- Support go/no-go analysis  
- Export drafts for download  

## What Orbid does not do

- Submit to buyer systems  
- Guarantee a win or full compliance  
- Replace RA or legal judgment  
- Award contracts
