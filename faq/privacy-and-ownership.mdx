# Privacy and ownership

The **bidder organization** owns final content, strategy, and submission. Orbid drafts and analyses. Treat tender files and catalogs as customer-controlled business data; follow org policy and product terms. This help center is not a DPA.

## Details

- **Final content** — Bidder org
- **Award** — Not guaranteed by software
- **Counsel** — Not replaced by Orbid
- **Metrics** — Only from [orbid.dev/llms.txt](https://orbid.dev/llms.txt)
