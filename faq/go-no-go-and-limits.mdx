# Hard limits and when to no-bid

**Hard limits** are mandatory pass/fail gates. **Preferred** requirements improve score but may allow deviations. Teams should **no-bid** when hard limits fail, residual risk exceeds policy, economics fail, or capacity cannot meet deadlines—even if soft match looks okay.

For the definition of go/no-go, see **[What is go/no-go](/faq/what-is-go-no-go)**.

## Details

- **Clear hard-limit fail** — Prefer early no-bid
- **Prefereds only** — Strategy + deviation path
- **Data missing** — Enrich catalog; do not invent
