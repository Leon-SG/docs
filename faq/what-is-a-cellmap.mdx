# What is a cellmap in bid automation?

A **cellmap** is a machine-readable map of a tender workbook: sheets, requirement rows, and response cells. Automation uses it to know where requirements live and where bidder answers must be written. Without a reliable cellmap, fill tools risk writing the wrong columns or skipping mandatory rows.

## Details

### What a cellmap usually captures

- Sheet roles (primary tech, accessories, service…)  
- Requirement vs instruction vs header rows  
- Response columns (device details, yes/no, source)  
- Anchors for multi-line requirements  

### Why it breaks

- Merged cells and decorative layouts  
- Repeated “note” blocks  
- Mixed languages in headers  

### Ops tip

Validate the cellmap on a sample of rows **before** bulk fill. Fixing map errors after fill is far costlier.
