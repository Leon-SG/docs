# What is bid fill (response form completion)?

**Bid fill** is populating the buyer’s response template—often Excel columns for device details, yes/no compliance, and source references—with values derived from the matched product and documentation. Fill quality depends on match quality and residual review; empty is better than invented.

## Details

### Fill inputs

- Locked product identity  
- Match outcomes + evidence  
- Cellmap (where to write)  

### Fill outputs

- Draft buyer workbook  
- Residual queue (empty required, weak evidence, conflicts)  

### Quality rules

- **No silent invention** — Audit and contractual risk
- **Evidence columns honest** — Evaluator verification
- **Residuals visible** — Human time on real risk
