# Knowledge vs memory?

**Knowledge** is shared workspace material (playbooks, past packs, approved text) the agent can draw on. **Memory** captures preferences and notes that improve continuity across sessions. Neither replaces the **product catalog** specs nor the **uploaded tender**—those remain primary evidence for match and fill.

## Details

- **Knowledge**
  - Best for: Reusable company docs, SOPs
  - Not for: One-off tender PDFs only in session
- **Memory**
  - Best for: Style, defaults, “how we bid” notes
  - Not for: Inventing product certifications
- **Library / catalog**
  - Best for: Specs used in formal match
  - Not for: Buyer submission
