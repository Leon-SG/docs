# Matching, deviations, and evidence

Orbid compares structured tender parameters to product specifications and surfaces meet / partial / gap style outcomes with deviations for review. **Do not** rely on AI matching alone when catalog data is incomplete or clauses need counsel. Prefer evidenced values; residuals beat invention.

## Details

- **Formal match** — After product lock; not rank alone
- **Deviation** — Documented difference vs requirement
- **Evidence** — Datasheet / cert / catalog field
- **Human** — Always review before submit
