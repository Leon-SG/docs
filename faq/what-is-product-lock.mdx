# What is product lock?

**Product lock** means you choose **one** catalog model as the candidate for **formal** matching against the tender. Until a product is locked, ranking or search is only a shortlist—not a compliance comparison. Switching models usually means rematch (and often re-fill).

## Details

- **Catalog search / rank** — Shortlist candidates—not formal compliance
- **Lock product** — Commit one model for formal match
- **Formal match** — Parameter comparison → deviations
- **Branch / switch** — Evaluate another model → rematch

## Anti-patterns

- Treating rank order as “we comply”  
- Filling Excel before lock when formal evidence is required  
- Mixing two models’ specs into one response without rematch
