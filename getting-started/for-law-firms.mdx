As a lawyer, you review each new matter, send a quote, do the work after the client pays and deliver the finished documents. You decide at every step.

## Before you start

- Sign in on the lawyer sign-in page with the work email on your invitation. If you have no invitation, ask your firm's administrator.

## The four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the new matter">
    Open **Engagement review**, read the client's matter and check the identity documents. Then select **Accept matter** or **Decline matter**. See [Review engagements](/law-firms/review-engagements).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a quote">
    After you accept, fill in the quote and select **Send quote**. See [Send a quote](/law-firms/send-a-quote).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the work">
    Once the client pays, you can open the matter workbench. Check the facts and files, edit documents and ask the client for more if you need it. See [Matter workbench](/law-firms/matter-workspace).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver to the client">
    Check every file, then select **Deliver** and confirm. The client sees nothing until you do. See [Deliver documents](/law-firms/deliver-documents).
  </Step>
</Steps>
