As a client, you send a matter, prove who you are, pay the firm's quote and receive your documents. Sending a matter is free. You pay only after the firm says yes and sends a price.

## Before you start

- An email address you can open. Orbid sends you a one-time code to sign in. There is no password.
- Identity documents for you or your company, as PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC files.

## The five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send your matter">
    Sign in with your email code and select **New matter**. It opens in a new tab. Follow the five steps: Choose service, Describe needs, Review details, Verify identity and View progress. Orbid asks guided questions one at a time. See [Submit a matter](/clients/submit-a-matter).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prove who you are">
    Upload the identity documents the lawyer asks for, then select **Submit and lock**. See [Upload identity documents](/clients/identity-documents).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the firm's answer">
    The lawyer reads your matter and accepts or declines it. The progress bar shows where you are. See [Track your matter](/clients/track-your-matter).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the quote and pay">
    The firm sends a fixed price. Check it, then select **Confirm & pay**. See [Quotes and payment](/clients/quotes-and-payment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your documents">
    Download your files and select **Confirm receipt**. See [Receive documents](/clients/receive-documents).
  </Step>
</Steps>
