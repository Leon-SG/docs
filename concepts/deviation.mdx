# Deviations in bid responses

A **deviation** is a stated difference between tender requirements and the offered product response—often documented in a deviation letter or matrix notes.

## Everyday

After match: ask for `deviation summary`, then generate a deviation letter if your process requires it.

## Professional notes

- Classify hard limit fails vs preferred misses.  
- Never hide deviations; buyers and compliance teams need transparency.
