# Compliance matrix

A **compliance matrix** maps tender requirements to product responses and status (meet/partial/gap/deviation) for review and submission packs.

## How Orbid helps

After match, ask to `generate compliance matrix` via [`run_generate_deliverable`](/guides/skills/deliverables). Humans verify statuses and evidence.
