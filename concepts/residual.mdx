# Residuals in bid fill

A **residual** is a response cell that remains empty, uncertain, or weakly evidenced after automated fill. Good process makes residuals visible for humans.

## Everyday

After “fill excel”, review anything still blank or flagged before export/submit.

## Professional notes

- **Missing catalog data** — Enrich product specs, re-match/fill
- **Ambiguous tender wording** — Human interpretation + note
- **Commercial/pricing lines** — Commercial owner fills
- **Hard limit fail** — Strategy / no-bid / deviation letter
