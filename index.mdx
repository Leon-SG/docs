# Orbid AI documentation

**Orbid AI (formerly MedStrato)** is a **native AI agent for medical-device bid work**. You open a session, give Orbid tenders and product context, and **delegate a job in plain language**. Orbid runs a tool loop—reading the package, matching a product, flagging gaps, filling forms, drafting deliverables—while **you own go/no-go, pricing, sign-off, and buyer submission**.

> **Delegate the work. Own the judgment.**
