These questions preserve uncertainty rather than invent product facts. They do not imply the product lacks a feature. Source inspection supports the documented baseline; deployment, policy, and product-owner confirmation are still needed for the items below. [S01]

| ID | Question or conflict | Affected pages and impact | Needed confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q02 | Counsel knowledge/memory/gaps/quality are specified but routes are placeholders | [Knowledge](/product/knowledge), [counsel](/product/counsel), [overview](/product/overview): no usable standalone workflow established | Feature owner, implementation status, enabled flags, and rollout plan |
| Q04 | File support and retention are not fully established | [Files](/product/files): upload and extraction expectations | Supported formats, size limits, indexing, deletion, and retention |
| Q09 | Internal routes are not a supported external API specification | [Overview](/product/overview), [settings](/product/settings) | Public API scope, version, authentication, schemas, rate limits, and support policy |

| Q11 | Intake v1/v2 coexist; specification and shipped copy differ | [Intake](/product/intake), [workflows](/product/workflows) | Active version, supported services, required documents, signing authority, and localized UI/consent labels |

## How to resolve an item

Record the responsible owner, dated evidence, affected release/environment, and resulting decision. Update the English page and this list first, then update all three complete translations and the relevant [source record](/product/sources). Keep unresolved items explicit until evidence resolves them. No release date or commercial commitment should be inferred from this list.
