Orbid AI supports client intake and legal work between clients and counsel. Clients provide information and documents; counsel reviews engagements, works on matters, and delivers results. Availability depends on the deployment. [S01]

## Who this is for

Clients, counsel, and authorized firm administrators use the workflows described here.
