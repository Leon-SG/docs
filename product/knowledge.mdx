## Counsel placeholders and specified intentions

The presence of a navigation item or UI specification does not mean the described operations can be performed.

The UI specification proposes reviewed knowledge versions, scoped and source-linked memory, prioritized gaps, and delivery-quality checks. These are **specified intentions pending implementation confirmation**. No release date is established. Do not instruct users to create or confirm records on these placeholder pages.

Matter-specific facts, document requests, and review findings in the [counsel workspace](/product/counsel) are separate implemented workflows; they do not establish a functioning standalone gaps or knowledge module. See [shared evidence rules](/product/rules).

Separately, the inspected research generation path can invoke a workspace-scoped knowledge-source reader when that connector is requested. The reader may return unconfigured, empty, or error rather than usable text. This internal read path does not establish an available standalone knowledge-management page, complete source coverage, or deployed availability; verify any sources returned. [S46]
