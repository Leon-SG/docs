Client intake collects business context, facts, documents, and identity materials before counsel decides whether to accept an engagement. The inspected code contains intake v2 alongside a legacy v1 flow. This page describes v2 and identifies the important difference in payment timing. [S15]

## Start and submit intake v2

For a pending firm invitation, sign in with the intended client company before opening it. In the inspected flow, the first eligible company to accept the invitation binds it; if an invitee email is specified, it must match the signed-in email. A revoked or expired link cannot be used. If the link is unavailable or has been bound to the wrong company, contact the firm to resolve it. [S29]

1. Open the firm's invitation or intake entry and sign in with the appropriate client organization account.
2. Select the relevant business/service and provide the requested direction and identity information.
3. Explain the matter, answer follow-up questions, and supply supporting files.
4. Review and correct the assembled facts, then submit them to proceed to verification.
5. Upload the required identity materials, review the consent text, and provide the signer information requested by the form.
6. Submit and lock the intake for the firm, then continue to the [client workspace](/product/client).

The source-defined v2 steps are `start → onboard → ask → confirm → verify → desk`. There is **no fee or payment step in v2 intake itself**. Engagement quotes and payment happen later through the [engagement flow](/product/engagements). Legacy v1 has scope/payment steps; do not apply its sequence to v2.

## Identity and access rules

The selected business determines the requested verification scope. Uploading documents does not mean counsel has marked them as passed. Issuing a quote requires every required KYC item to pass and its files to be available. The inspected acceptance action does not enforce this gate. This is a product workflow description, not a legal assessment of which verification law applies to your case.

For the inspected KYC upload flow, files may be PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP, or HEIC/HEIF, up to **25 MiB per file** and **10 files per checklist item**. If the resumable upload service is unavailable, the multipart fallback has a lower **10 MiB per file** limit. Empty files and PDFs detected as encrypted are rejected; changing a filename alone does not change the file’s detected type. These limits apply to identity materials, not every upload in Orbid. The resumable KYC upload path also checks a **300 MiB** total against stored files in the intake session; other files in that session count toward it. [S20]

The inspected intake upload paths may require file-processing consent, AI-processing consent, or acknowledgment of applicable notices before a file can be uploaded. Read the text shown for your case and complete the requested steps if you agree; if you do not agree or cannot proceed, contact the firm. A blocked upload is not confirmation that a file was sent. [S38]

Before lock, v2 token access requires the appropriate client session and ownership checks. An invitation can require the invited email; a mismatch must be resolved by signing in with the intended account or contacting the firm. A token alone is not a universal permission grant.

## Errors and locked information

The inspected v2 verification submission can pause when uploaded identity files have not finished processing or processing failed. Check the item status and follow the displayed guidance; contact the firm if it remains unresolved. A parsed file may still require a lawyer’s manual check and is not automatically KYC-approved. [S40]

If the declaration was saved but the lock failed, the inspected client retries certain temporary failures automatically, and a subsequent submission can retry the lock for an intake that has never been locked. Check the displayed status before assuming handoff succeeded; if the error persists, contact the firm and provide the request ID if shown. This recovery does not replace the separate declined-intake resubmission process. [S40]

If mandatory materials are missing or a save/upload fails, resolve the reported item before submitting. Check the saved state before leaving. After facts are submitted, the earlier explanation and confirmation steps may be viewed read-only while verification is pending. Submitting verification locks and releases the intake to the firm; do not assume those earlier steps remain editable. A declined engagement has a controlled reopen/resubmit path described under [engagements](/product/engagements).

Business-service coverage, exact required documents, signer authorization, localized consent wording, and which intake version is enabled remain [Q11](/product/open-questions). UI copy differs between the specification and implementation; this manual describes actions without inventing button labels.
