Apply these rules when reviewing matter information, source documents, and generated work. [S01]

## Evidence and human responsibility

Keep source files and versions with each result. Check source passages, record unresolved facts, and ask the responsible person when evidence is missing or ambiguous. Counsel owns legal review and approval.

## Changes invalidate assumptions

When facts or documents change, review affected findings and drafts against the updated material. Earlier outputs may refer to an older version.

## Access and routes

Paths shown in this manual identify product routes, not documentation links. On a dedicated product host, clean paths such as `/counsel` may be used; on shared hosts the physical path is `/orbid/counsel`. Use your organization's entry link rather than guessing a hostname. [S01]

A visible navigation item does not establish permission or availability. Client organization membership, matter visibility, firm assignment, and the relevant action's server-side checks affect access. Do not use a different account to bypass a denial. See [settings](/product/settings) and [client workspace](/product/client).

## Failures and partial results

- Upload or parsing failure: check the file and retry the failed step. A stored file does not prove successful extraction.
- Unknown facts or unsupported claims: supply evidence or retain the gap; do not turn uncertainty into a positive answer.
- Stale state or conflicting action: reload the latest state before retrying. Quote acceptance and engagement decisions use state/version checks.
- Generation or download failure: inspect the returned status and available artifact before retrying.
- Access denial: check the signed-in organization, invitation, workspace, and matter assignment with the responsible administrator.

No universal file-size limit, retention policy, subscription allowance, response-time guarantee, or regional availability is established here. Those remain [open questions](/product/open-questions), rather than implied product promises.
