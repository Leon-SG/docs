Settings routes provide entry points for organization, account, access, and usage configuration. Their existence does not establish that every account can edit every setting or that every connector is active. [S19]

Use the settings entry in the current workspace. For the counsel workspace, the shared-host route map uses `/orbid/settings` with these section slugs; dedicated hosts can use a different prefix:

Open the relevant section, inspect the options available to your role, make a permitted change, and verify that it saved. Do not assume hidden controls are available through a direct URL. Client-specific settings have routes under `/orbid/client/settings`, but the inspected pages return not found when the client-settings feature is disabled. Their availability and editable controls depend on configuration and permissions; they do not imply access to firm administration. [S25]

## Team and invitations

Where the bulk-invitation control is available to an administrator, enter up to **50** addresses, select the offered member or administrator role, and review the per-address results. The inspected flow processes invitations individually, so one request can be partly successful; invalid addresses, seat limits, or identity-service unavailability can leave failures. The form retains failed entries for correction. An invitation sent is not proof that the recipient has joined. [S45]

The inspected fine-grained member roles panel is hidden when its feature switch is off. When it is available, only an administrator can change the non-admin roles offered for that organization; the panel does not change an administrator’s organization role. Confirm the intended permissions and saved result with an administrator rather than inferring them from a role name. [S47]

In the inspected organization-membership path, a request is refused if the member list read for that request identifies the target as the last administrator. The check and identity-service updates are separate steps, so do not rely on it to coordinate concurrent changes; change administrators one at a time and verify the resulting list. If a role change or removal reports a failure, reload the member list and confirm the resulting state before retrying: these operations involve multiple identity-service steps and can fail partway through. Contact an administrator if the state is unclear. [S51] See [Q17](/product/open-questions).

## Connectors

In the inspected firm connector settings, an authorized editor chooses enabled regions and default sources. Saving these preferences does not establish that a connector is configured or reachable. When no valid default remains and the firm region is enabled, the parser falls back to the `knowledge` default; clearing the last default therefore does not necessarily leave an empty selection. Verify the saved settings and actual source status; do not treat the checkbox as an access-control guarantee. [S49]

## Notification preferences

Where the notifications section is available, select the events and email or in-app channels shown, save, and verify the saved state. Preferences are stored per user, with firm and client settings kept separately. In the inspected code, unspecified choices default to enabled. The reviewed email sender applies preferences only to mapped events; other milestone emails may still be sent, and a preference lookup failure allows sending. A saved preference is not a guarantee of delivery or suppression for every notification. [S26]

## Access and ownership

### Audit records

The inspected audit view is not a complete activity log: audit writes are best effort, access-denied events are sampled, and a failed matter-ledger read can leave only organization events in the result. CSV export is capped at **5000** rows. Use its filters and pagination where available, and verify important events against the responsible system or administrator rather than assuming a missing entry proves no action occurred. [S50]

### Client company identity

In the inspected client company settings, an administrator changing the legal name, registration number, or jurisdiction causes the server to flag those changed fields for KYC re-review. Saving the profile is not confirmation that the re-review has finished. A legal-name change also attempts to synchronize display names, but that synchronization can fail; verify the displayed name and contact the administrator or firm if it remains inconsistent. [S44]

### Matter access

In the inspected matter-access settings, a client organization administrator can choose `all` or `members` visibility. The extra member restriction only blocks access when the server enforcement mode is on; off and shadow modes do not enforce it. Confirm the deployed mode before relying on this setting. [S25, S43]

The firm settings form lets an administrator save default matter members, but its own help text says automatic addition to new matters is for a later release. A saved default is therefore not evidence that anyone was added to a particular matter. Check the matter’s actual membership and access. [S43]

Client organization identity, firm workspace assignment, matter visibility, and specific action gates are separate concerns. Engagement review and document work can require different states. A navigation entry is not an authorization rule. Ask your administrator to resolve a mismatch rather than changing unrelated organization data to gain access.

The complete role/permission matrix, invitation lifecycle, connector prerequisites, policy effects, audit coverage, and allowed administrative changes remain [Q15](/product/open-questions).

## Billing and downloads

The inspected client billing view reads at most **500** company matter records. Its displayed payment amount and currency come from the last invoice found for that matter, when one exists, rather than directly from a provider transaction; the payment list and amount may therefore be incomplete or differ from the charge. The listed card endings describe previously used methods, not a card vault. Reconcile with the agreed quote and provider record, and contact the firm about discrepancies. [S48] See [Q16](/product/open-questions) for the unresolved reconciliation question.

Product plan information belongs on [orbid.dev/pricing](https://orbid.dev/pricing). This manual does not infer prices or allowances from source constants. Client engagement payment follows its own quote; verify the operation and amount before confirming.
