---
title: "Engagement review, quotes, and payment"
description: "Follow acceptance, quoting, payment, and controlled resubmission rules."
---


An intake handoff is not an accepted, paid engagement. Counsel reviews the locked submission, decides acceptance, and issues a quote; the client then reviews the quote and pays through an enabled provider. [S16]

## Counsel review

1. Open the engagement queue at `/orbid/counsel/engagements` using an authorized firm account.
2. Inspect the locked intake, identity checklist, and available documents.
3. Review required KYC items. Before issuing a quote, all required items must be marked passed and their files must be available. The inspected acceptance action does not enforce this gate.
4. Accept or decline the engagement. A decline needs a client-facing reason of at least **10 characters**; the internal note is separate and is not shown to the client by the engagement view.
5. After acceptance, prepare and issue the quote. Acceptance and quote issuance are distinct steps.

The source lifecycle includes `pending_review → accepted → quoted → paid`. Decline enters `declined`; controlled reopening uses `reopened` before resubmission. Matter status and engagement status are different fields and need not share the same names.

A quote draft can be saved during review, after reopening or acceptance, or while preparing a replacement. Saving a draft does not change the engagement status or notify the client. Issuing it is a separate action: the engagement must have been accepted, and the required KYC and file checks must pass. The issued quote replaces the saved draft; a sent quote is replaced by issuing a new version rather than withdrawing it. [S16]

## Client quote and payment

Review the scope, amount, currency, validity, and quote version in the client workspace. The source default validity is **14 days**; counsel can set **1–60 days**. Supported quote currency identifiers in the model are `EUR`, `USD`, and `CNY`; this does not establish that every provider accepts every currency.

In the inspected fixed-price flow, the client cannot decline a quote through the quote action. If the terms need to change or the quote expires, contact the firm; counsel can issue a replacement with a new version. Do not confuse this with counsel declining the engagement. [S16]

A fixed-price confirmation can accept the quote and start payment together. The server obtains the payable amount and currency from the quote, rather than trusting browser-supplied values. A failed payment may leave the quote accepted so the client can retry. Verify the returned payment status rather than treating a click as a successful charge.

Provider configuration distinguishes real and simulated payment paths. A local mock payment is not evidence of a production charge. Do not enter or rely on test-payment instructions as a production procedure. The inspected engagement flow opens the v2 counsel workbench after the engagement is paid, rather than immediately after acceptance.

A successful payment does not by itself prove that the background work has started or finished. The inspected code records payment and then attempts to enqueue eligible work; a dispatch failure does not undo the payment result. If payment succeeded but work does not appear, check the matter status and contact the firm rather than making another payment. [S32]

If no online payment method is available, contact the firm to confirm how to proceed. A displayed provider name alone does not establish that the deployment is using a real payment provider; check the payment screen and ask the firm if the mode is unclear. [S34]

## Exceptions and recovery

In the inspected online payment flow, starting payment creates a payment request; a pending request is not a successful payment. A request can expire independently of the quote. If the screen reports that payment timed out, check the latest payment and quote status before starting again; if the outcome is unclear, contact the firm before attempting another payment. A current, valid quote is still required. [S34]

Expired or replaced quotes need a current version; reload before acting on a stale quote. Concurrent engagement changes can return a stale-state or assignment conflict. Do not repeat an action blindly. In the inspected lifecycle, a declined intake can be resubmitted at most **two times**; check the remaining availability in the client view.

To revise a declined intake, open the matter in the client workspace and use the reopen action when offered. Update the information or files and check the KYC list. Before resubmitting, supply a file for each required KYC item and enter the signer name requested by the interface. Resubmission records a new signature, locks the intake again, and returns it to counsel for review. If the limit is reached or an action is unavailable, contact the firm. [S27]

Firm visibility, workspace assignment, and operator privileges affect review access. Full refund/cancellation rules, deployed payment methods, tax treatment, currency coverage, and client authorization remain [Q12](/product/open-questions).
