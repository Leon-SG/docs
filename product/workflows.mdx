## Client-to-counsel workflow

1. **Describe the request.** The client signs in, selects the business, explains the matter, and checks facts through [intake v2](/product/intake).
2. **Provide identity materials.** The client uploads required materials and submits/locks the intake; upload is distinct from counsel's KYC pass decision.
3. **Review engagement.** Authorized counsel examines the locked submission and verification items, then accepts or declines it through [engagement review](/product/engagements).
4. **Quote and pay.** After acceptance, counsel issues a quote. The client reviews its current version, scope, and validity in the [client workspace](/product/client), then pays through an enabled provider.
5. **Prepare legal work.** Counsel opens the paid v2 matter in the [document workspace](/product/counsel), checks facts and sources, reviews drafts and findings, and handles information requests.
6. **Deliver and receive.** Counsel uses the authorized delivery path. The client inspects the received files and acknowledges when available.

A successful payment does not guarantee that background work was dispatched. If the work does not appear, check the matter status and contact the firm before paying again. [S32]

A declined intake uses its controlled reopen/resubmit path. A stale quote needs refreshing. A placeholder counsel knowledge/gaps/quality page is not a required workflow step. The legacy v1 intake payment sequence differs; verify the active version before following instructions.

## Role handoffs

Clients confirm supplied facts and respond to requests; counsel decides acceptance, professional content, and delivery. Follow [shared rules](/product/rules) whenever evidence or task state changes.
