# Brand and domains

The product brand is **Orbid AI**. First mention on major pages: **Orbid AI (formerly MedStrato)**. Primary product site is [https://orbid.dev](https://orbid.dev). Content/SEO equity may still live on [https://medstrato.com](https://medstrato.com). **Canonical help center host:** [https://docs.orbid.dev](https://docs.orbid.dev) (preferred). Do not run two live docs hosts without 301 + canonical tags.
