# Trust, evidence, and human judgment

Orbid accelerates bid prep with grounded tools. **Your organization owns judgment, pricing, regulatory sign-off, and buyer submission.** Prefer empty residuals over invented values.

## Ownership split

- **Scan and structure tender files** — Confirm ambiguous legal/commercial clauses
- **Match product specs to requirements** — Lock product strategy and accept deviations
- **Draft fills and documents** — Approve content before submit
- **Flag residuals and gaps** — Fill or accept residual risk
- **Support go/no-go analysis** — Make the bid / no-bid call

## Evidence discipline

- Prefer values tied to datasheets, certificates, or catalog fields  
- If the agent cannot evidence a cell → residual, not fiction  
- See [Evidence & ownership](/concepts/evidence-and-ownership)  

## Safety defaults

- No award guarantees  
- No silent substitution of product models  
- Re-scan after file replace  
- Re-match after material spec corrections  

## Privacy baseline

Treat tenders and catalogs as customer-controlled business data. Follow your org policy and product terms. This help center is not a DPA.
