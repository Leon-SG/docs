# Common workflows

Use these recipes when you know the **outcome** you want. Each page: what you provide, what to say, what Orbid does, what you review.

- **[Scan a tender](/guides/workflows/scan-a-tender)** — Structured view of requirements and deadlines
- **[Match and review gaps](/guides/workflows/match-and-review-gaps)** — Product vs tender comparison + deviations
- **[Decide go / no-go](/guides/workflows/decide-go-no-go)** — Decision support—not auto-award
- **[Fill Excel](/guides/workflows/fill-excel)** — Buyer form draft with residuals visible
- **[Generate deliverables](/guides/workflows/generate-deliverables)** — Matrix, deviation letter, packages

Also see [What to say](/guides/what-to-say) for prompt patterns.
