# Go/No-Go checklist

Use a structured checklist for bid/no-bid. Orbid’s `run_gonogo` supports analysis; **humans decide**.

## Checklist

1. Hard limits: registration, local presence, mandatory certificates  
2. Technical coverage after formal match  
3. Deviation tolerance vs buyer rules  
4. Commercial viability (pricing ownership human)  
5. Capacity and delivery risk  
6. Strategic value vs opportunity cost  
7. Deadline feasibility  

## Utterances

- `should we bid on this`  
- `Go/No-Go`
