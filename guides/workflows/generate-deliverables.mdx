# Generate bid deliverables

Deliverable generation drafts compliance matrices, deviation letters, proposals, or multi-file packages from matched tender and product context. Drafts still need human legal/RA review and your sign-off before buyer submission.

You want draft artifacts for the bid pack—compliance matrix, deviation letter, technical proposal, or a multi-file package.

You provide completed match for most artifact types; clear artifact type in your ask.

## What to say

```text
Generate a compliance matrix for this tender and product.
```

```text
Draft a deviation letter covering the gaps from the match.
```

```text
List the deliverable types you can produce, then generate a technical proposal draft.
```

```text
生成合规矩阵和偏差函草稿，我来审。
```

## What Orbid does

1. Lists types if you are unsure  
2. Generates a **single** artifact or a **package** of several  
3. Returns drafts for download / further edit  

Do not invent public “N types” KPIs in your own docs—use what the product lists in-session.

## What you review

- Status labels on matrix rows (meet / partial / gap)  
- Deviation language vs legal/RA policy  
- Consistency with the filled Excel  
- Final approval before buyer submission
