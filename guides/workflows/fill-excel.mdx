# Fill the buyer Excel form

Fill writes matched product data into the buyer Excel form where the cellmap and evidence allow, and leaves **residuals** empty or flagged when evidence is weak. You review blanks, commercial rows, and export for submission **outside** Orbid.

You want a filled buyer workbook draft grounded in matched product data—not invented cells.

You provide match completed for one product; tender source is **xlsx/xls** with form structure (cellmap).

## What to say

```text
Fill the buyer Excel form from the match. Flag anything you could not evidence.
```

```text
把匹配结果填回招标 Excel，标出无法举证的格子。
```

```text
Re-fill after I corrected product specs.
```

## What Orbid does

1. Writes bidder-response values into mapped cells when data exists  
2. Leaves **residuals** empty or flagged when evidence is weak  
3. Provides download of the draft workbook  

Note: match may already have auto-filled once—check session downloads before re-running.

## What you review

- **Residuals / blanks** — Empty beats invented—see [Residual](/concepts/residual)
- **Hard technical claims** — Must match datasheet / catalog
- **Commercial rows** — Usually human-owned
- **Wrong sheet / map** — Re-scan if files changed

Then export, package, and **submit outside Orbid** as required.
