# What is Orbid AI?

**Orbid AI (formerly MedStrato)** is a **native AI agent** for medical-device bid operations. You work in an **Agents session**: upload tender packages, speak in natural language, and Orbid uses tools to scan, match, assess, fill, and draft. **Your organization owns judgment and submission.**

## Identity

- **Product name** — **Orbid AI**
- **Formerly** — **MedStrato**
- **Kind of product** — Native **bid agent** (session + tools + deliverables)
- **Primary site** — [https://orbid.dev](https://orbid.dev)
- **Typical users** — Bid, product, and regulatory specialists at OEMs and distributors
- **Human ownership** — Content strategy, go/no-go, pricing, legal/RA sign-off, buyer submission

## What “native agent” means here

Orbid is **not** a static form wizard, a generic chatbot, or software that awards contracts by itself.

It **is** a conversation plus tool loop for medical-device bids: domain tools for tenders, catalogs, matrices, and Excel fill, with **decision support**, evidence, and residuals for humans to own.

Orbid can call tools such as tender scan, product match, go/no-go, Excel fill, and deliverable generation. You do not need to memorize tool ids for daily work—see [What to say](/guides/what-to-say). Technical names are in [Tools reference](/guides/skills).

## What Orbid optimizes for

- Turning multi-sheet tender packages into structured requirements  
- Matching a **locked** product to those requirements  
- Surfacing deviations and residuals instead of inventing specs  
- Drafting bid artifacts for human review  

## What Orbid is not

- A guarantee of award  
- A replacement for local counsel or authorized signatories  
- Perfect match when catalog data is missing  
- A general office assistant outside bid workflows
