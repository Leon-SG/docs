Administrators decide who sees matters in **Matter access**. Client companies choose between everyone and selected people. Firms choose default members for new matters.

## Before you start

- You must be an administrator.

## Client companies

Under **Who can see matters**, choose one option, then select **Save**.

| Option | Who sees a matter |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone in the company | All members |
| Only people added to the matter | Admins, plus people who created it or were added |

## Law firms

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Matter access">
    In your settings, select **Matter access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose default members">
    Under **Default members for new matters**, pick the colleagues.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**. Orbid adds these people to every new matter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Firm admins and the responsible lawyer can always open a matter.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A colleague cannot open a matter | Add them to the matter, or check the company setting. |
| “Only admins can do this” | Ask an administrator. |
| “Your sign-in has expired” | Sign in again. |
