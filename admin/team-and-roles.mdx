Administrators can invite up to 50 people at once. Choose a role, send the invitations and check the result.

## Before you start

- You must be an administrator.
- Have the email addresses ready, one per line or separated by commas.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the people page">
    In your settings, open **Team & members** (firms) or **Members** (client companies).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add addresses">
    Under **Invite several people**, enter up to 50 email addresses. Choose **Member** or **Admin** next to "As".
  </Step>
  {/* screenshot: settings-team-invite | Team & members page with the "Invite several people" box holding three addresses and the "Send invitations" button; demo firm "Muster Rechtsanwälte" | role=admin | url=/orbid/settings/team */}
  <Step title="Send and read the result">
    Select **Send invitations**. Orbid shows how many were sent and how many were not. Addresses that failed stay in the box so you can fix them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Addresses per invitation | 50 |

## Which roles are there?

Admin, Partner, Associate, Paralegal, Finance, Approver, Requester and Viewer. Admin is set with the Member or Admin switch. Which roles you can pick depends on your account. An invitation sent does not mean the person has joined yet.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Not a valid email” | Fix the address. |
| “Already a member” or “Already invited” | Nothing to do. |
| “Seat limit reached” | Ask your Orbid contact to add seats. |
| “Could not send” or “Try again later” | Try again in a few minutes. |
| “At most 50 at a time” | Send the rest in a second batch. |
| “Only an admin can see this” | Ask an administrator. |
