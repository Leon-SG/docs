In **Notifications**, you choose which events reach you by email and which inside Orbid. Your choices apply to you only.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">
    In your settings, select **Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose events">
    For each event, switch **Email** and **In Orbid** on or off.
  </Step>
  {/* screenshot: settings-notifications | Notifications settings with the events list and Email and In Orbid switches; one event switched off; demo lawyer account | role=lawyer | url=/orbid/settings/notifications */}
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**, then reload the page to check your choices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which events are there?

Every event is on by default for both Email and In Orbid. Your choices apply to you only.

### For law firms

| Event | Email | In Orbid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A matter is assigned to me | On | On |
| The client replies | On | On |
| Requested documents arrive | On | On |
| The client accepts a quote | On | On |
| A payment is received | On | On |

### For clients

| Event | Email | In Orbid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A quote is ready | On | On |
| The lawyer replies | On | On |
| The lawyer asks for documents | On | On |
| A payment is due | On | On |
| A document is delivered | On | On |

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Something went wrong” | Try again. |
| You still get an email | Wait a few minutes, then reload and check your choices were saved. |
