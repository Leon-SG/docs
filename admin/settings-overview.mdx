Open **Settings** from the menu in your workspace. What you can change depends on your role.

## Law firm settings

- Firm profile
- Connectors
- Team & members
- Agent policy
- Plans & billing
- Account
- Agent preferences
- Language
- Usage
- Matter access
- Notifications
- Audit log

## Client settings

Your firm may need to turn client settings on first. You may then see:

- Profile
- Preferences
- Notifications
- Sign-in & security
- Company profile
- Members
- Matter access
- Billing
- Audit log

Only admins can change company details. Client settings do not give access to the firm's settings.

## What if I change company details?

If you change the legal name, registration number or country, the company's identity check goes back to review. Saving does not mean the review is done.

## Where are invoices?

Client companies see invoices and payments in **Billing**. Prices are on [orbid.dev/pricing](https://orbid.dev/pricing).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A section is missing | Your role or your firm's setup may hide it. Ask an administrator. |
| “Only admins can change this” | Ask an administrator. |
