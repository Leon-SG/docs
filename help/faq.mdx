Short answers to common questions. Each links to a full guide.

## Clients

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does it cost anything to send a matter?">
    No. You pay only after the firm accepts and sends a quote. See [Submit a matter](/clients/submit-a-matter).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I sign in?">
    Enter your email and then the one-time code Orbid sends you. There is no password. See [Submit a matter](/clients/submit-a-matter).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which identity files can I upload?">
    PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC, up to 25 MB each and 10 files per item. See [Identity documents](/clients/identity-documents).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my PDF not accepted?">
    Empty files and password-protected PDFs are not accepted. Remove the password and upload it again. See [Identity documents](/clients/identity-documents).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the firm need my identity documents?">
    The lawyer must know who the client is before starting work. See [Identity documents](/clients/identity-documents).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my matter after I submit?">
    Not while the lawyer is reviewing it. If the firm declines, you can edit and resubmit. See [Resubmit a declined matter](/clients/resubmit-a-declined-matter).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long is a quote valid?">
    14 days by default. The firm can choose 1 to 60 days. See [Quotes and payment](/clients/quotes-and-payment).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which currencies are possible?">
    EUR, USD and CNY. See [Quotes and payment](/clients/quotes-and-payment).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I decline a quote?">
    No. The price is fixed. Contact the firm if you want changes. The lawyer can send a new quote. See [Quotes and payment](/clients/quotes-and-payment).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My payment failed. What now?">
    Check your quote and payment status first. If you are unsure, ask the firm before you pay again. See [Quotes and payment](/clients/quotes-and-payment).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not see my documents yet?">
    You see files only after the lawyer approves and delivers them. See [Receive documents](/clients/receive-documents).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many times can I resubmit a declined matter?">
    Two times. See [Resubmit a declined matter](/clients/resubmit-a-declined-matter).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not see a matter my colleague can see?">
    Your company may limit a matter to certain people. Ask your administrator. See [Matter access](/admin/matter-access).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Nothing happens when I select New matter. Why?">
    Orbid opens it in a new tab. If your browser blocked it, allow pop-ups for this site and select **New matter** again. See [Submit a matter](/clients/submit-a-matter).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Law firms

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long must a decline reason be?">
    At least 10 characters. See [Review engagements](/law-firms/review-engagements).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not send a quote?">
    You must accept the matter, and every required identity item must have passed. See [Send a quote](/law-firms/send-a-quote).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I take back a quote I sent?">
    No. Send a new quote. It replaces the old one. See [Send a quote](/law-firms/send-a-quote).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to comments when I deliver?">
    In the export menu, choose **Keep comments (n)** or **Remove all comments**. Then choose the **Lawyer version** (internal notes and notes for both) or the **Client version** (notes for the client and for both). Select **Deliver** and confirm. See [Deliver documents](/law-firms/deliver-documents).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which files can I upload to the contract library?">
    DOCX, DOC, PDF, TXT and MD, up to 50 MB each. See [Contract library](/law-firms/firm-library).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the AI pass identity checks or deliver files?">
    No. Orbid's AI is an aid. The lawyer decides. See [Matter workbench](/law-firms/matter-workspace).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can change how long library files are kept?">
    Only firm administrators. They can choose 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 or 10 years, or Indefinitely. Deletion runs overnight and licensed documents are not affected. See [Contract library](/law-firms/firm-library).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Administrators

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many people can I invite at once?">
    Up to 50. See [Team and roles](/admin/team-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many rows can I export from the audit log?">
    Up to 5,000 rows per CSV file. See [Audit log](/admin/audit-log).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
