These are the words you see in Orbid.

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Matter | One piece of legal work, with its facts, files and messages. |
| Intake | The first steps, where the client describes the matter and uploads identity documents. |
| Engagement | The deal between client and firm: review, acceptance, quote and payment. |
| Engagement review | The firm's list of new matters waiting for a yes or no. |
| Quote | The firm's fixed price, with items, currency and an end date. |
| KYC | “Know your customer”. The identity checks a lawyer does before starting work. |
| Your edited version | Your own copy of a document, where you make changes. The original stays as it was. |
| Matter workbench | The lawyer's workspace for a paid matter, with tabs for Files, Chat, Agent, Review, KYC, Facts, Asks and Deliveries. |
| Delivery | The step where the lawyer selects **Deliver** and confirms, so the client receives the finished files. |
| File library | The firm's store of documents and clauses you can search. Its page title is Contract library. |
| XiaoOu | Orbid's AI assistant for clients. A lawyer still decides. |
| Submit and lock | The last button when a client sends a matter. After that, the answers are locked. |
